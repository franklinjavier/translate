# Compact translation popover

Approved by Frank in this chat on 2026-10-15.

The translation appears in a black rounded popover, approximately 220–480 pt wide. Short text uses a small card. The translation leads; the language and Copy/Replace controls sit in a quiet footer. The original stays hidden. Long content wraps and scrolls at a height limit. Keyboard shortcuts and source-app focus remain intact.

Prefer above the selection, flip below near the screen top, and clamp horizontally to the active display visible frame. An arrow points to the selection even after horizontal adjustment. Hide the arrow when the user drags the panel or when a truthful attachment cannot be drawn. Mouse fallback remains available for apps without AX selection bounds.

Verification covers short/long content, loading/error states, top/bottom/side collisions, multiple displays, arrow alignment, and actual hosting-view sizing. Codex independently builds, tests, inspects rendered screenshots and installs the signed app.
